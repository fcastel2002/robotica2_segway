The MP1584EN mini buck converter module is a compact DC-DC step-down voltage regulator designed to power microcontrollers and modules from higher-voltage supplies. Ideal for Arduino, ESP32, robotics, LEDs and embedded builds, it provides reliable, efficient regulation in space‑constrained projects.

Why choose this module

  • Powered by the MP1584EN IC for dependable, efficient conversion compared with linear regulators
  • Available as 3A adjustable or fixed outputs: 3.3V, 5V, 9V and 12V
  • Small footprint (approx. 22 × 17 × 6 mm) fits tight enclosures and portable designs
  • Great for common use cases like a 12V to 5V buck converter for microcontrollers and sensors
  • Suitable for Arduino/ESP32 dev boards, Raspberry Pi accessories, sensors and LED strips

Specifications

  • Converter type: DC-DC buck (step-down)
  • Regulator IC: MP1584EN
  • Output options: 3A adjustable; fixed 3.3V, 5V, 9V, 12V
  • Module size: approx. 22 × 17 × 6 mm
  • Typical applications: power regulation for Arduino, ESP32, robotics, LEDs and embedded electronics

Getting the best results

  • Select the correct fixed or adjustable version for your project before powering your load.
  • For adjustable versions, set the output with a multimeter before connecting electronics.
  • Allow reasonable current derating and airflow for continuous high‑current operation.
  • Use this as a step‑down (buck) converter only; it is not a buck‑boost regulator.

If you need a compact, efficient buck converter module for clean 3.3V, 5V, 9V or 12V rails, the MP1584EN offers a proven, maker‑friendly solution.
